Troubleshooting basics

A quantity didn't sync

  1. Check that the product is linked to an Etsy listing on the matching screen. Items without a match are not synced.
  2. Check whether the listing is marked unsupported. The App skips listings it can't update without risking other fields, and shows them in the list.
  3. Check that your Etsy connection is still active. If Etsy access was revoked or expired, reconnect your shop from the App's settings.
  4. Check your plan's SKU limit. Items above the limit aren't synced until you upgrade or unlink other items.
  5. Allow a few minutes: Etsy stock is checked every few minutes, so changes made on Etsy can take a short while to appear in Shopify.

Quantities differ between Shopify and Etsy

Open the daily mismatch report in the App. It lists every linked item whose quantities differ and lets you choose which value to apply.

A change looks wrong

Open the audit log, find the change, and use Rollback to restore the previous quantity. Rollback is available for 30 days after a change.

Disconnecting or uninstalling

You can disconnect an Etsy shop from the App's settings or uninstall the App from your Shopify admin at any time. Neither action changes your Etsy listings. To have your data deleted sooner, email us.
