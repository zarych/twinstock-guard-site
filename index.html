TwinStock Guard

Inventory-only Etsy & Shopify sync. Never edits your listings.

TwinStock Guard keeps your stock quantities identical across your Shopify store and your Etsy shop, so you stop overselling. It changes one number per item: quantity. Everything else stays exactly as you made it.

The problem

Two storefronts, one shelf of stock

When you sell the same handmade or vintage items on both Etsy and Shopify, a sale on one side doesn't reduce stock on the other. Sooner or later you sell an item you no longer have — then comes the refund, the apology and the hit to your shop's reputation.

Why existing tools worry sellers

Sync shouldn't rewrite your shop

Many sync tools treat one platform as the “master copy” and push everything across. Sellers report prices, titles, photos and SKUs being overwritten, and variations changed — on listings they spent hours perfecting.

What it does

Keeps quantities in sync — in both directions

  • A sale or stock change in Shopify updates the matching Etsy quantity.
  • A sale or stock change on Etsy updates the matching Shopify quantity.
  • Works per variation, so each size or colour keeps its own count.
  • Matches products by SKU automatically and lets you link the rest yourself.

What it never does

  • Change prices
  • Change titles
  • Change photos
  • Change descriptions
  • Change SKUs
  • Change variations
  • Delete listings
  • Read buyer or order data
Safety first

Built so you can check every change

Dry-run preview

Before the first sync, see exactly which quantities would change. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Audit log & rollback

Every change is recorded with before and after values, and can be undone with one click.

Daily mismatch report

Once a day, the app compares both sides and lists any quantities that don't match, so drift never goes unnoticed.

Safe disconnect

Disconnecting or uninstalling makes zero changes to your Etsy listings. They stay exactly as they are.

How it works

Three steps to synced stock

  1. Install

    Add TwinStock Guard to your store from the Shopify App Store.

  2. Connect your Etsy shop

    Sign in to Etsy and grant access to your listings' inventory. Products are matched by SKU, and you review the rest.

  3. Review and confirm

    Check the dry-run preview of every quantity change. Syncing starts only after you confirm.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

What data does TwinStock Guard access?

From Shopify: your products, variants and inventory levels. From Etsy (through Etsy's official sign-in): your listings and their inventory. That's all the app needs to match items and keep quantities equal.

It does not request access to orders, receipts, transactions or customers, and it never reads buyer names, addresses or emails. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will it ever change my prices, titles or photos?

No. The only value TwinStock Guard writes is the stock quantity. Prices, titles, photos, descriptions, SKUs and variations are left untouched, and every quantity change is logged so you can verify it and undo it.

What happens when I uninstall?

Syncing stops immediately, and the app makes no changes to your Etsy listings — nothing is deleted or deactivated. Your stored data, including Etsy access tokens, is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy. Paid plans are cancelled through Shopify.

Does it work with Etsy listings that use international (domestic/global) pricing?

Safety comes first. If the app can't update a listing's quantity while guaranteeing that every other field — including its prices — stays exactly the same, it marks that listing as “unsupported”, shows it to you, and doesn't write to it. You'll always see which listings are and aren't being synced.

How fast is the sync?

Shopify changes are sent to Etsy shortly after Shopify reports them, usually within a minute or two. Etsy stock is checked every few minutes and changes are applied to Shopify on the next check. The daily mismatch report catches anything that slipped through.

Which product is the “source of truth”?

Neither side overwrites the other. The app applies stock changes from each side to the other, so a sale anywhere reduces your stock everywhere. On first setup, the dry-run preview shows you any existing differences before anything is changed.
